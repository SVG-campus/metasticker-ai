## 1. Physical Sticker Manufacturing & Material Specifications

To ensure the physical product feels like a luxury Apple or Nintendo collectible—not cheap promotional paper—MetaSticker uses a **5-layer composite architecture**:

```
[Layer 1]  3.2 mil UV-Resistant Matte or Gloss Protective Over-Laminate
              (Waterproof, dishwasher safe, scratch resistant, 3-year outdoor life)
[Layer 2]  300–350gsm Cast Vinyl with Rainbow Holographic Diffraction Foil
              (High-gloss metallic foil that shimmers under direct light)
[Layer 3]  NTAG213 / NTAG215 NFC Micro-Inlay (144–504 Bytes)
              (Pre-encoded with Cloudflare Anycast edge URL: https://metasticker.app/{code})
[Layer 4]  Permanent Solvent-Based Acrylic Adhesive
              (Strong bond on laptops, aluminum bottles, phone cases; leaves zero residue when peeled)
[Layer 5]  90# Heavy Bleached Kraft Waxy Backing Paper (Silicone Release Liner)
              (Backing paper features high-contrast QR code, POSA security watermark, and scratch seal)
```

3. **RangeMe (Target & Walmart Supplier Portal):**
   * RangeMe is the verified software platform used by retail category merchants at Target and Walmart to review new consumer packaged goods (CPG).
